Driving Test Cancellations UK

Privacy policy — effective 2026-09-09

Driving Test Cancellations UK (“the extension”) helps you watch your own UK driving test booking session for earlier appointments.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension:

  • Collects no personal data, licence numbers, booking references, or browsing history
  • Has no server, no analytics, and no telemetry of any kind
  • Stores your filter settings and watcher state only on your own computer, in your browser’s local extension storage
  • Never sees or handles your login details — you log in to the government booking service yourself, in your own browser session

How it works

The extension operates inside the tab where you are already logged in to the UK government driving test booking service. It re-checks the results page you have open, and when a slot matching your filters appears, it navigates to it and holds it. It never confirms a booking — you always review and confirm yourself.

Data sharing

None. No data leaves your browser.

Third-party services

If you purchase the optional premium upgrade, payment is processed by Stripe via ExtensionPay. We never receive your payment details. Premium status is checked against the payment provider when the extension starts.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this URL and reflected in the extension’s store listing before release.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing support link.
